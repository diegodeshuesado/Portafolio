operation:/< Create fictional stories in the streets of a given neighbourhood.

You choose a neighbourhood, take a random walk and photograph façades, streets and urban landscapes that catch your eye, marking on the map where each photo was taken. From those places you write a fictional story: the street names, what people tell in the neighbourhood or pure invention can be the starting point.
