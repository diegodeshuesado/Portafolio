operation:/< Rewrite poetry with asemic writing.

You choose a poem —by taste, by chance or one of your own— and write it without letters, line by line: you let the wrist move and the stroke slide while meditating on or reading aloud the line being written. This writing holds a particular fascination for being unusual and mysterious, as it invites us to find hidden meanings in it.
