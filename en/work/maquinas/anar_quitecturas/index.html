operation:/< Intervene images of monuments, façades or objects that represent power.

The intervention is made with words, phrases, images and stickers: like graffiti, a slogan painted on a wall or a meme. You photograph a monument, a façade or an object of power —or choose a free image in the machine’s search engine—, intervene it with lines and text, name it and, if you like, add a slogan and place it on the map.
