Collage

Series
Apaseo el Alto
Year
2019
Works
7 works

Paper, scissors, newspaper clippings, glue and markers were the means used to engage with a context of violence and insecurity, and with the things that surround a city in transition. Collage is used to cut and paste, mark and erase, and build an image of the surroundings; in this case, Apaseo el Alto.
