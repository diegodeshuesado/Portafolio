Sculptures modelled in ceramic paste, glazed and kiln-fired. These pieces were made at the Universidad Autónoma de Querétaro in 2019. The artist’s own physical expression and characteristic features served as inspiration, creating a dialogue between the material, the artist and what the work aims to be.
